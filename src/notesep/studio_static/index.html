The spectrogram is the original audio; colored bands mark each score note’s fundamental and timing. Cross-check with another transcription model to surface disagreements and possible omissions; gold outlines mark notes worth reviewing, not proven errors. Click a band to preview it in context. Drag notes left/right for timing, up/down for pitch, or pull the right edge for duration. After separation, compare the direct sum with the mixture and audition individual notes; gold outlines then mark quiet, pitch-inconsistent, or competing-pitch outputs. Neither a clean note check nor a low sum residual proves that no notes are missing or assigned incorrectly.
